## 样章 1 — seg6（引言：参考工作定位）· L116–133

**原文**
> The companion scheme studied in this work~\cite{arXiv2510} perturbs a subset of the semantic latent codes and shapes the perturbation with a discriminator so that the released noise stays close to a Laplace draw while remaining semantically mild; this is a reasonable engineering trade-off between legitimate distortion and privacy. That work \emph{does} state a nominal privacy budget: it reports a global sensitivity $\Delta f=351.88$ and calibrates Laplace noise $\mathrm{Lap}(0,\Delta f/\varepsilon)$ for $\varepsilon\in\{1,5,\ldots,2000\}$. The gap is therefore not the absence of a parameter but the absence of an \emph{auditable guarantee for the noise actually released}: the discriminator learns a pattern that only approximates a Laplace draw, so the published $(\varepsilon,\delta)$ value is not a proof about the released mechanism, and no identification-error statement is made. This work does not replace that design; it supplies a framework to audit any such release under standard DP metrics and to check it against an identification attack. For comparison we re-price a \emph{Gaussian proxy} of its operating point (Section~\ref{sec:experiments}); this quantifies how the budget should be charged if the pattern were an exact additive Gaussian mechanism, and is not a claim about the pattern's true distribution.

## 样章 3 — seg13（相关工作：领域共性缺口）· L224–242

**命中问题**：P2 超长句（71 词）；分号并列 + "however" 转折套叠；降重高风险（领域背景陈述句）。

**原文**
> \emph{Semantic representations and their privacy.} Modern generative encoders decompose an image into a global structure code and part-wise local codes that support controllable synthesis and editing~\cite{semanticstylegan}. Releasing such codes over a wireless link is the standard semantic-communication pipeline, and the released part codes carry the identity of the subject; recent surveys of security and privacy in semantic communications cover the threat models and defence techniques~\cite{semcomsurvey_won,semcomsurvey_meng}. The reference semantic-adaptive DP design~\cite{arXiv2510} perturbs a subset of codes with Laplace noise calibrated to a stated global sensitivity ($\Delta f=351.88$) and nominal $\varepsilon$, then shapes it with a learned pattern to keep Bob's distortion low; however, neither the \emph{component-wise} sensitivity of the decomposed code nor the composition law across private blocks is made explicit, and the learned released noise carries no auditable DP proof. Many semantic-privacy works likewise optimise a learned noise shape against reconstruction distortion (e.g.\ LPIPS) without evaluating an identification attack or its error floor. \emph{Gap:} the common deficiency is an auditable, block-wise account paired with an operational attack bound, not the mere presence of an $\varepsilon$ value. The present paper starts from the component sensitivity and the composition law instead.

> \emph{Semantic representations and their privacy.} A modern generative encoder splits an image into a global structure code and part-wise local codes, which is what makes controlled synthesis and editing possible~\cite{semanticstylegan}.
